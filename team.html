XPixel Group


XPixel团队介绍


XPixel (/eks-pɪksel/) 建立于2019年，致力于让世界变得更清晰和美好。我们关注计算机视觉中的诸多问题，包括但不限于图像与视频的超分辨率、去噪、去模糊等。我们的成员主要来自中国科学院深圳先进技术研究院的研究人员与学生，亦与多家合作单位开展学术交流与联合研究；合作方包括索菲亚大学INSAIT、上海人工智能实验室、香港中文大学、南洋理工大学、澳门大学、腾讯和商汤科技等机构。我们聚焦底层计算机视觉，力求将研究成果转化为可落地的优秀技术。团队文化：奉献、专注与平衡。

XPixel (/eks-ˈpɪksəl/) group was established in 2019 with a primary focus on tackling low-level vision problems, including but not limited to image/video super-resolution, denoising, deblurring, and more. We strive to examine images and video across all pixels. Our members are primarily researchers and students at SIAT (Shenzhen), Chinese Academy of Sciences; we also pursue academic exchange and joint research with partner institutions, including INSAIT(Bulgaria), Shanghai AI Lab, CUHK (Hong Kong), NTU (Singapore), UM (Macao), Tencent, and SenseTime, among others. We devote ourselves to low-level vision, aiming to translate our research into practical, high-quality technology for the real world. Our Team Culture: LOVE-FOCUS-BALANCE!
