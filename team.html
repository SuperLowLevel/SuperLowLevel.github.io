XPixel Group


XPixel团队介绍


XPixel (/eks-pɪksel/) 建立于2019年，致力于让世界变得更清晰和美好。我们关注计算机视觉的各种问题，包括但不限于图像/视频超分辨率、去噪、去模糊等。我们的成员是来自中国科学院深圳先进技术研究院的研究人员和学生。我们致力于底层计算机视觉问题的研究，旨在将我们的工作成果转化为现实世界的优秀技术。团队文化是奉献、专注和平衡!

XPixel (/eks-ˈpɪksəl/) group was established in 2019 with a primary focus on tackling low-level vision problems, including but not limited to image/video super-resolution, denoising, deblurring etc. We strive to examine images and video footages across all pixels. Our members comprise researchers and students at SIAT (Shenzhen), Chinese Academy of Sciences. We devote ourselves to research and development on low-level vision problems, aiming to translate the outcome of our work into solving real-world obstacles. Our Team Culture: LOVE-FOCUS-BALANCE!
