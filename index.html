General Intelligence & Large Model Series

底层视觉之美

The Beauty of Low-Level Vision — AI Behind High-Definition Films

Chao Dong  &  Jinfan Hu

Publishing House of Electronics Industry  ·  Broadview

More than a technical manual — this is a cross-disciplinary dialogue between science and aesthetics. Written in narrative prose by first-line researchers, the book traces the full arc of low-level vision: from the handcrafted algorithms of early computer vision to the intelligent flowering of deep learning, from laboratory breakthroughs to real-world deployment. Dense concepts become vivid stories, and research journeys become genuine conversations — making AI's quest to "make the world clearer" accessible to everyone.
