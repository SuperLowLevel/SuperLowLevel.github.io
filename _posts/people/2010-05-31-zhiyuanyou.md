Zhiyuan You is currently a joint PhD candidate in MMLab, CUHK and SIAT, CAS. He is supervised by Prof. Chao Dong and Prof. Tianfan Xue, and also work closely with Dr. Jinjin Gu. He received his M.Eng. and B.Eng. from SJTU, supervised by Prof. Xinyi Le and Prof. Yu Zheng. He previously conducted research at several leading institutions, including Bytedance Seed, Adobe, Shanghai AI Lab, Horizon Robotics, and SenseTime. His current research interest mainly lies in generation models, multi-modal systems, vision-centric agent, and low-level vision.
