Yu Tang is a first-year PhD student under the supervision of Prof. Chao Dong, and works closely with Prof. Jinjin Gu. He received his Master's degree from Renmin University of China in 2023 and his Bachelor's degree from Northeastern University in 2020. Prior to his doctoral study, he worked as an Imaging Algorithm Engineer at Honor from 2023 to 2025. His current research interests lie in low-level vision and video editing.
