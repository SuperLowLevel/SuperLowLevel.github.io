Zheyuan Li is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Macau, advised by Prof. Jiantao Zhou, and currently serves as a Research Intern at the Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology (SIAT). His research interests lie in low-level vision, with a focus on developing frameworks and network architectures for image super-resolution. Prior to his current doctoral studies, Zheyuan worked as a full-time Research Assistant at SIAT within the XPixel group, under the supervision of Prof. Chao Dong and Prof. Yu Qiao. He received his B.E. in Software Engineering from Northwestern Polytechnical University in 2022.
